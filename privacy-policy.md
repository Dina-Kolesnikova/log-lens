# Privacy Policy — Log Lens (JSON Log Viewer)

Log Lens does not collect, store, transmit, or share any user data.
It runs entirely locally in your browser.

- No analytics or tracking of any kind
- No network requests are made by the extension
- No data is sent to any server, developer, or third party
- The only data stored is the user's own settings (auto-run site
  list and appearance preferences), saved locally via Chrome's
  storage API and never transmitted anywhere
